Introduction

Live Oil Prices ("we", "us", or "our") operates liveoilprices.com (the "Site"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Site.

Information We Collect

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous usage statistics including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Referring website and search terms
  • General geographic location (country/city level)

This data is aggregated and anonymized. We do not collect personally identifiable information through Google Analytics.

Advertising (When Enabled)

When advertising is enabled on the Site, we use Google AdSense to display advertisements. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Show ads based on your prior visits to our Site or other websites
  • Measure ad effectiveness and provide aggregate reporting
  • Prevent the same ad from continuously reappearing

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Cookies

The Site uses cookies for analytics and advertising purposes. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us improve your experience. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Site.

Third-Party Services

We work with the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Web analytics (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdSense: Advertising platform (when enabled) (Privacy Policy)
  • Pyth Network: Real-time market data (Privacy Policy)

Data Usage

We use the collected data to:

  • Improve the Site's content and user experience
  • Understand how visitors use the Site
  • Display relevant advertisements (when enabled)
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Disable cookies in your browser settings

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete such information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the information available on our Site.

Note: This Site does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or account credentials. We only collect anonymous usage statistics and advertising interaction data as described above.
